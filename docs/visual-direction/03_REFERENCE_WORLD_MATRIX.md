# Reference World Comparison Matrix

Status: initial comparison baseline

The six reference worlds exist to stress-test the visual language. They should not converge toward one generic definition of "alien."

| World | Primary identity | Dominant silhouette | Atmosphere | Life | Human adaptation | Ship-scale hook |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Khepri | barren crater/fracture moon | crater rings + branching trenches | none | none | crater-wall/subsurface installations | crater dives, trench runs, hidden hangars |
| Pelagos | dense float biosphere | horizontal suspended layers | dense, hazy, electrical | abundant | elevated/braced/beacon-led structures | instrument flight through fog and floating ecology |
| Nadir-Tau | tidally locked climate world | long lateral bands/ridges | strong circulation | concentrated in twilight band | linear terminator settlements | cross hot/twilight/cold zones in one flight |
| Vitria | silica storm world | wind-aligned ribs and blades | abrasive dust storms | sparse | lee-side/recessed facilities | storm shelter routes and wind-cut corridors |
| Thalassa | oceanic archipelago | vertical island spines over broad water | humid/stormy | mainly marine/coastal | cliff/floating/tidal infrastructure | sea arches, calderas, platform approaches |
| Eden-9 | rare familiar biosphere | valleys, mountains, mature canopy | Earth-like | abundant/familiar roles | open long-term settlement | free flight through habitable terrain |

## Distinction test

Each concept should remain distinguishable when reduced to:

- grayscale;
- one horizon silhouette;
- one ship-approach screenshot;
- one ground-level screenshot;
- one POI exterior.

If two worlds become visually interchangeable under those conditions, their structural rules need revision.

## Coverage provided by the six concepts

The set intentionally spans:

- no atmosphere -> very dense atmosphere;
- sterile -> highly biological;
- hostile -> comfortable;
- vertical -> horizontal dominant forms;
- open visibility -> severe visibility limits;
- geology-driven -> ecology-driven identity;
- sparse human presence -> mature civilian settlement;
- low-altitude precision routes -> broad exploratory flight.

## What is not yet represented

Future concepts may explore:

- true cryogenic chemistry worlds;
- subsurface-primary biospheres;
- megastructure/ruin-dominated worlds;
- very high-gravity environments;
- low-density super-Earth atmospheres;
- ring worlds / artificial habitats if technically and thematically appropriate;
- highly volcanic or magma-dominated environments;
- planet-scale biological structures;
- worlds where the primary exploration layer is underground.

These should be added only if they introduce genuinely new structural rules rather than filling a checklist.

## Selection for first visual iteration

The first concept-image round should prioritize three worlds:

1. **Khepri** — validates barren-world identity and ship-scale terrain.
2. **Pelagos** — validates a strongly non-Earth biosphere.
3. **Vitria** — validates geology/weather-driven alienness without lush life.

If these three can be made visually distinct and compelling, the broader direction is probably sound. Nadir-Tau, Thalassa, and Eden-9 can then test whether the system remains flexible rather than becoming one aesthetic recipe.
