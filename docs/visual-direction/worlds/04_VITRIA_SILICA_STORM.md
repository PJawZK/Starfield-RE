# Vitria — Silica Storm World

Status: reference world / geology-weather concept

## Purpose

Vitria explores a world where geology and weather are inseparable. It should feel hostile and alien without requiring biological abundance.

## Core physical rules

- dry atmosphere carrying abrasive silicate particles;
- persistent high-velocity winds;
- frequent electrically charged dust fronts;
- exposed mineral layers slowly laminated and polished by storms;
- large pressure-shadow regions where finer material accumulates;
- sparse life, if any, concentrated in sheltered pockets.

## Shape language

Primary form: long parallel mineral ribs aligned with prevailing wind.

Secondary form: leaning blade formations and eroded fins.

Detail form: laminated glassy surfaces, scalloped abrasion marks, fractured translucent edges.

Void form: narrow wind slots, deep lee-side hollows, protected channels.

Directional bias: extremely strong wind alignment.

## Horizon read

The horizon should look combed by the atmosphere. Large ridges and blade fields point in common directions rather than scattering randomly.

Storm fronts appear as opaque moving walls with lightning or charge effects embedded inside them.

## Ecology

Biology is optional and sparse. If present, it should exploit shelter:

- organisms beneath overhangs;
- mineral-feeding mats in protected seams;
- pressure-shadow growths;
- dormant forms exposed only after storms.

The world should prove that geological alienness can carry a planet even without lush ecology.

## Palette and light

Mineral translucency and specular variation may be important, but the primary identity should remain the aligned rib/blade silhouette. Storms drastically alter contrast and visibility.

## Human adaptation

Structures are built behind or within natural windbreaks:

- low-profile exterior volumes;
- sacrificial abrasion plating;
- recessed hangars;
- protected intakes;
- deeply buried service corridors;
- sensor masts on exposed ridge ends;
- landing approaches from the lee side.

## Ship-scale gameplay

The wind-cut terrain naturally creates flight corridors:

- high-speed runs between long ribs;
- sheltered low passes during storms;
- dangerous crosswind exposure above ridges;
- hidden installations in pressure shadows;
- navigation by terrain alignment when sensors degrade.

## Signature location — Glass Maw

A mining installation occupies a huge eroded slot between two mineral fins. The visible surface buildings are mostly sacrificial industrial structures; the valuable processing complex sits deep in the protected rock.

During storms, the only safe ship approach follows a winding lee-side channel.

## Alienness target

Vitria should be recognizable from the directional geology alone, even with neutral lighting and no weather effects.

## Failure mode to avoid

Do not turn the planet into a generic crystal biome with randomly placed giant crystals. The mineral forms must arise from coherent alignment, erosion, layering, and weather.
