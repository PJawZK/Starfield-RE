# Pelagos — Dense-Atmosphere Float Biosphere

Status: reference world / biosphere concept

## Purpose

Pelagos explores a life-bearing world that is biologically rich but does not default to Earth-like forests, grasslands, or familiar vertical tree silhouettes.

## Core physical rules

- dense atmosphere;
- high humidity and suspended aerosols;
- moderate gravity;
- frequent electrical storms;
- low direct visibility at ground level;
- strong prevailing winds at altitude;
- buoyancy is ecologically important.

## Shape language

Primary form: huge horizontal biological canopies and suspended colonies.

Secondary form: broad fan/ribbon flora bent by persistent wind.

Detail form: porous, membrane-like tissues and layered aerodynamic surfaces.

Void form: fog-filled gaps beneath floating mats and between giant horizontal growths.

Directional bias: horizontal and wind-swept rather than vertical.

## Horizon read

The horizon should rarely resemble a terrestrial tree line. Large buoyant organisms or colonial growths hang at different heights in haze, producing layered silhouettes that fade into atmospheric depth.

Ground forms are lower and broader, shaped by wind loading.

## Ecology

Life exploits the dense atmosphere:

- drifting filter-feeders;
- tethered buoyant colonies;
- broad low plants that harvest diffuse light;
- electro-sensitive organisms reacting to storms;
- predators that use fog layers and vertical currents.

The ecosystem should feel adapted to pressure, wind, and low visibility rather than decorated with exotic shapes.

## Palette and light

Diffuse light, little hard shadow, strong depth falloff. Color may be unusual, but the world should remain distinctive when desaturated because its identity comes from layered atmosphere and horizontal biological forms.

Electrical storms should create intermittent silhouette flashes rather than constant neon ambience.

## Human adaptation

Settlements use:

- elevated platforms above unstable or saturated ground;
- heavy bracing;
- short-range beacons;
- recessed or shielded landing facilities;
- lightning protection;
- large instrument masts;
- enclosed connections rather than long exposed walkways.

## Ship-scale gameplay

Flight should feel different because visibility is limited.

Potential gameplay:

- instrument-assisted low-altitude navigation;
- emerging suddenly into clear pockets;
- flying beneath or around giant suspended organisms;
- storm fronts obscuring pirate traffic;
- sensor contacts appearing before visual identification;
- vertical routes between atmospheric layers.

## Signature location — The Lantern Chain

An abandoned atmospheric-research complex consists of several elevated platforms linked by beacon towers. During storms, only the nearest beacon is visible, turning approach into a navigation problem.

A hidden smuggler station has repurposed one lower platform beneath the normal traffic layer.

## Alienness target

Pelagos should feel alive, but never like a rainforest with strange colors.

## Failure mode to avoid

Do not cover every organism in emissive spots. Bioluminescence, if present, should be selective and functional rather than the primary alien cue.
