# Nadir-Tau — Tidally Locked Terminator World

Status: reference world / climate-structure concept

## Purpose

Nadir-Tau explores a planet whose identity comes from a permanent global climate geometry rather than a single biome palette.

## Core physical rules

- tidally locked to its star;
- permanent day side, permanent night side;
- narrow habitable twilight/terminator belt;
- powerful atmospheric circulation from hot side to cold side;
- extreme lateral temperature gradient;
- life and settlement concentrated in moving or layered climate bands.

## Shape language

Primary form: long horizon-parallel ridges and terraces tracking climate bands.

Secondary form: wind-carved escarpments and layered ice/mineral transitions.

Detail form: directional erosion, frost/mineral laminations, wind-shaped growths.

Void form: deep lee-side valleys, thermal caverns, shadowed fault corridors.

Directional bias: strong lateral banding around the planet.

## Horizon read

The environment should communicate directional climate. One side of the view trends toward harsh brilliance and heat shimmer; the other toward deep cold and darkness. The habitable strip is visually squeezed between extremes.

The world should look different when facing starward versus nightward even from the same location.

## Ecology

Life occupies the twilight band and exploits constant gradients:

- migratory organisms follow temperature/pressure bands;
- low, wind-resistant flora aligns with atmospheric flow;
- some organisms extend roots or burrows toward warmer subsurface zones;
- night-facing life may rely on geothermal or chemical energy.

## Human adaptation

Settlements cluster along thermal sweet spots and often extend linearly along the terminator rather than radially.

Architecture includes:

- thermal shielding on starward faces;
- radiators/cold sinks toward nightward sides;
- wind barriers;
- buried service corridors;
- observatories and power infrastructure on the bright edge;
- cryogenic industry on the dark edge.

## Ship-scale gameplay

Flight can cross meaningful climate layers quickly:

- fly starward into intense glare and turbulence;
- descend into sheltered twilight valleys;
- cross into frozen night-side canyons;
- track caravans or settlements aligned along the terminator;
- use the permanent sun direction for navigation.

## Signature location — Meridian Spine

A long human settlement follows a geological ridge parallel to the terminator. One side contains power collectors and heat-resistant industry; the other contains radiators, storage, and cryogenic processing.

A hostile group occupies abandoned tunnels beneath the ridge and uses the night-side exits for concealed ship approaches.

## Alienness target

Nadir-Tau should feel like a world where geography and climate have a permanent direction. The player should intuitively learn that moving starward and moving nightward are fundamentally different choices.

## Failure mode to avoid

Do not represent tidal locking merely by giving one biome warmer colors and another colder colors. The gradient must influence terrain, atmosphere, ecology, architecture, and traversal.
