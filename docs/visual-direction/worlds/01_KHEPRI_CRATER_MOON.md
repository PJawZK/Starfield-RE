# Khepri — Fractured Crater Moon

Status: reference world / flight-first concept

## Purpose

Khepri is the control environment for ship-scale terrain and the planned Crater Run concept. It should prove that an airless body can feel distinctive without relying on vegetation, weather, or saturated color.

## Core physical rules

- airless, low-gravity moon;
- ancient heavily cratered surface;
- later tectonic/fracture activity cut through older impact basins;
- large subsurface voids and collapsed lava/impact tubes;
- extreme light/shadow contrast;
- exposed regolith and glassy impact melt fields.

## Shape language

Primary form: enormous nested crater rings.

Secondary form: long branching fracture trenches that cut crater walls.

Detail form: sharp regolith shelves, impact-glass veins, fractured slabs.

Void form: collapsed tubes, shadowed fissures, crater-wall cavities.

Directional bias: radial crater geometry intersected by branching linear fractures.

## Horizon read

Khepri should read as broad, monumental, and quiet rather than cluttered. One or two giant crater rims dominate the skyline. Fractures create visible cuts through otherwise smooth regolith expanses.

The world must remain visually strong in grayscale.

## Palette and light

Restrained materials: charcoal, pale mineral dust, dark impact glass, metallic inclusions. Identity comes primarily from hard lighting, deep shadow, and geometry.

Low sun angles should create kilometer-scale shadow shapes.

## Human adaptation

Settlements prefer:

- crater walls for shielding;
- fracture interiors for concealment and structural access;
- subsurface voids for habitation;
- rim positions for sensors and defenses.

Exterior structures should be sparse and functional.

## Ship-scale gameplay

Khepri is designed around flight routes:

- diving over crater rims;
- traversing deep radial trenches;
- following fractures to hidden installations;
- low passes beneath overhangs/collapsed shelves;
- entering large artificial/natural cavities where technically feasible.

## Signature location — The Scar

A gigantic fracture cuts through an older crater. From orbit it appears geological. At low altitude, artificial lights and heat signatures reveal a pirate installation excavated into one wall.

Approach sequence:

```text
crater rim
-> steep descent
-> fracture corridor
-> sensor contact
-> surface defenses
-> narrow wall opening
-> concealed hangar / external landing shelf
```

This becomes the natural environment for the Crater Run prototype.

## Alienness target

Khepri is not biologically alien. Its job is to prove that barren worlds can still be memorable through geology, scale, darkness, and human adaptation.

## Failure mode to avoid

Do not fill the landscape with random rocks, wrecks, or glowing crystals simply to avoid emptiness. Large quiet areas are part of the visual identity.
